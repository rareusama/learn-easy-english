Privacy Policy

How this website handles visitor information.

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Learn Easy English provides beginner English lessons with Urdu support. This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit this website or contact us.

Information you provide

This website does not currently offer a visitor account or contact form. If you email us, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include in your message. We use it to read and respond to your enquiry.

Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as IP address, browser details and request data, as part of delivering and securing the website. Please refer to GitHub's privacy statement for information about its practices.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

We have not intentionally added Google Analytics or other visitor-tracking analytics to this website. If analytics or advertising services are added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant services and technologies.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled in the future, that service and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure and personalise advertising, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements and their own policies. No advertising service is currently promised or guaranteed on this website.

External websites

Our lessons may include links to other websites. We do not control those websites and their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for general English learning. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. Parents or guardians who have a concern may contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the website's features or services change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
