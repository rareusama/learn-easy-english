Welcome to Learn Easy English

Learn Easy English is an independent educational website created to help beginners understand and practise practical English with simple Urdu support.

What you will find here

  • Everyday English sentences with Urdu meanings
  • Beginner-friendly grammar explanations
  • Vocabulary and example sentences
  • Speaking practice ideas and study tips

Our approach

We aim to publish clear, useful and original learning material. Lessons are designed for practice and general education. We encourage learners to check important language usage in context and practise regularly.
