50 Daily Use English Sentences with Urdu Meanings

Learn 50 useful everyday English sentences with simple Urdu meanings and examples.

Daily use English sentences help beginners speak with confidence. Start with a few sentences every day, say them aloud, and use them in real conversations.

50 useful daily English sentences

These sentences are grouped by common situations. Read the English sentence, check the Urdu meaning, and practise saying it naturally. The Urdu translations are simple guides; the best translation can change with context.

How to practise these sentences

  1. Choose five sentences and read them aloud twice.
  2. Cover the Urdu meaning and try to remember the English.
  3. Change a word to make a sentence about your own life.
  4. Use one sentence in a real conversation when possible.
Practice task: Choose three examples from this lesson, write them in your notebook, and say them aloud. Try making one new sentence of your own.

Educational content only. Examples are for learning and practice.

Make these sentences your own

A sentence becomes easier to remember when you connect it to your own routine. Change the person, place or time while keeping the sentence pattern.

  • “I am getting ready.” → “I am getting ready for school.”
  • “I will call you later.” → “I will call my friend later.”
  • “Please speak slowly.” → “Please speak slowly because I am learning English.”

Speaking challenge: Choose five sentences from different groups. Say each one without looking, then use it in a short two-line conversation.
