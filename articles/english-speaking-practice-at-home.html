How to Practise English Speaking at Home

A realistic daily routine to practise English speaking at home, even as a beginner.

You can improve your speaking with short, regular practice. You do not need to memorise long speeches. Focus on useful sentences and clear pronunciation.

اردو میں: اس سبق کو آہستہ آہستہ پڑھیں، مثالوں کو بلند آواز سے دہرائیں اور روزانہ مشق کریں۔

A 15-minute daily routine

Minutes 1–5: read five short sentences aloud. Minutes 6–10: describe what you are doing. Minutes 11–15: record yourself and listen once.

Talk about your day

I woke up early.
میں جلدی اٹھا/اٹھی۔
I had breakfast.
میں نے ناشتہ کیا۔
I went to work.
میں کام پر گیا/گئی۔
I am learning English.
میں انگریزی سیکھ رہا/رہی ہوں۔

Stay consistent

Practise a little every day. Mistakes are a normal part of learning. Repeat useful phrases until they feel natural.

Practice task: Choose three examples from this lesson, write them in your notebook, and say them aloud. Try making one new sentence of your own.
