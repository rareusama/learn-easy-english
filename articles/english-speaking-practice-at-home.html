How to Practise English Speaking at Home

A realistic daily routine to practise English speaking at home, even as a beginner.

You can improve your speaking with short, regular practice. You do not need to memorise long speeches. Focus on useful sentences and clear pronunciation.

اردو میں: اس سبق کو آہستہ آہستہ پڑھیں، مثالوں کو بلند آواز سے دہرائیں اور روزانہ مشق کریں۔

A 15-minute daily routine

Minutes 1–5: read five short sentences aloud. Minutes 6–10: describe what you are doing. Minutes 11–15: record yourself and listen once.

Talk about your day

I woke up early.
میں جلدی اٹھا/اٹھی۔
I had breakfast.
میں نے ناشتہ کیا۔
I went to work.
میں کام پر گیا/گئی۔
I am learning English.
میں انگریزی سیکھ رہا/رہی ہوں۔

Stay consistent

Practise a little every day. Mistakes are a normal part of learning. Repeat useful phrases until they feel natural.

Try a short daily conversation

A: What did you do today?
آپ نے آج کیا کیا؟
B: I went to work and studied English.
میں کام پر گیا/گئی اور انگریزی پڑھی۔
A: What are you learning now?
آپ اب کیا سیکھ رہے ہیں؟
B: I am practising speaking.
میں بولنے کی مشق کر رہا/رہی ہوں۔

Use the listen-and-repeat method

Choose a short English audio or video made for learners. Listen to one sentence, pause, and repeat it. Try to copy the rhythm and clear sounds rather than speaking very fast. Repeat the same short clip several times.

Record yourself kindly

Record 30 to 60 seconds of your own speech. Listen once and choose just one thing to improve, such as a missing word or unclear sound. Do not worry about having a perfect accent. Clear communication is the goal.

A simple seven-day practice plan

  • Day 1: Introduce yourself in five sentences.
  • Day 2: Describe your room.
  • Day 3: Talk about your daily routine.
  • Day 4: Describe a favourite meal.
  • Day 5: Ask and answer five simple questions.
  • Day 6: Retell what you did yesterday.
  • Day 7: Record a one-minute talk and notice your progress.
Practice task: Choose three examples from this lesson, write them in your notebook, and say them aloud. Try making one new sentence of your own.
