WH Question Words in English with Urdu Meanings

Learn how to ask questions using what, where, when, who, why and how.

This beginner-friendly lesson explains the topic step by step. Read each example, notice the pattern, and then make your own sentences. Learning a little every day is more effective than trying to memorise everything at once.

اردو میں: ہر مثال کو غور سے پڑھیں، اس کا مطلب سمجھیں اور پھر اپنی طرف سے ایک نیا جملہ بنائیں۔

What

Use what to ask about a thing, action or information.

What is your name?
آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟

Where

Use where to ask about a place.

Where do you live?
آپ کہاں رہتے ہیں؟

When

Use when to ask about time.

When does the shop open?
دکان کب کھلتی ہے؟

Who

Use who to ask about a person.

Who is your teacher?
آپ کے استاد کون ہیں؟

Why

Use why to ask for a reason.

Why are you late?
آپ کو دیر کیوں ہوئی؟

How

Use how to ask about a way, condition or process.

How are you?
آپ کیسے ہیں؟
How do you go to school?
آپ اسکول کیسے جاتے ہیں؟

Basic question pattern

Many questions use a question word + helping verb + subject + main verb. Example: “Where do you work?” With the verb be, the order is often question word + be + subject: “Where are you?”

Quick practice

Make one question with each word: what, where, when, who, why and how. Try answering each question in a complete sentence.

Practice task: Write five original sentences using the rules in this lesson. Read them aloud, then check your spelling and grammar.

This lesson is for English-learning practice. Meanings can vary slightly with context.
