WH Question Words in English with Urdu Meanings

Ask clear everyday questions using what, where, when, who, why and how.

آسان وضاحت: Question words سوال پوچھنے کے لیے استعمال ہوتے ہیں۔ ہر لفظ کا اپنا مقصد ہے، جیسے جگہ، وقت، وجہ یا شخص کے بارے میں پوچھنا۔

1. What — کیا

Ask about a thing, action or information.

What is your name?
آپ کا نام کیا ہے؟

2. Where — کہاں

Ask about a place or location.

Where do you live?
آپ کہاں رہتے ہیں؟

3. When — کب

Ask about time or a date.

When does the shop open?
دکان کب کھلتی ہے؟

4. Who — کون

Ask about a person.

Who is your teacher?
آپ کے استاد کون ہیں؟

5. Why — کیوں

Ask for a reason. Answers often use “because”.

Why are you late?
آپ کو دیر کیوں ہوئی؟

6. How — کیسے / کس طرح

Ask about a way, condition or process. How can also combine with words such as many, much, old and often.

How are you?
آپ کیسے ہیں؟
How do you go to school?
آپ اسکول کیسے جاتے ہیں؟
How old are you?
آپ کی عمر کتنی ہے؟

7. Basic question patterns

With many action verbs: Question word + do/does/did + subject + base verb? Example: “Where do you work?”

With the verb be: Question word + am/is/are + subject? Example: “Where are you?”

For past questions, use did + base verb: “When did they arrive?”

8. Short conversation

A: Where do you live?
آپ کہاں رہتے ہیں؟
B: I live in Lahore.
میں لاہور میں رہتا ہوں۔
A: Why are you learning English?
آپ انگریزی کیوں سیکھ رہے ہیں؟
B: Because I want to improve my communication.
کیونکہ میں اپنی گفتگو بہتر بنانا چاہتا ہوں۔

Practice: choose the correct word

  1. ___ is your best friend? (Who / Where)
  2. ___ do you wake up? (When / Why)
  3. ___ are you happy? (Why / What)
  4. ___ do you spell your name? (How / Who)
  5. ___ is my notebook? (Where / When)
Show answers

1. Who · 2. When · 3. Why · 4. How · 5. Where

Speaking task: Write one question with each word and answer each question aloud in a complete sentence.

Question structure can vary depending on the verb and meaning. These patterns cover common beginner questions.
