Past Simple Tense in English with Urdu Examples

Learn how to describe actions that started and finished in the past.

آسان وضاحت: Past Simple ایسے کام کے لیے استعمال ہوتا ہے جو ماضی میں ہوا اور ختم ہو گیا۔

1. When do we use it?

Use the past simple for finished actions. Common time words include yesterday, last night, last week, in 2020 and two days ago.

2. Positive sentences

Structure: Subject + past form of verb + object.

I visited my friend yesterday.
میں کل اپنے دوست سے ملا۔
She cooked dinner.
اس نے رات کا کھانا پکایا۔
They went to the market.
وہ بازار گئے۔
We watched a film last night.
ہم نے گزشتہ رات ایک فلم دیکھی۔

3. Regular and irregular verbs

Many regular verbs add -ed: work → worked, clean → cleaned, play → played. Some verbs change completely and must be learned: go → went, eat → ate, see → saw, buy → bought, write → wrote.

Spelling tip: verbs ending in consonant + y often change y to ied: study → studied. But play → played.

4. Negative sentences

Structure: Subject + did not (didn't) + base verb.

I did not go to school.
میں اسکول نہیں گیا۔
He didn't call me.
اس نے مجھے فون نہیں کیا۔

Remember: After did/didn't, use the base verb: “didn't go”, not “didn't went”.

5. Questions

Structure: Did + subject + base verb?

Did you finish your work?
کیا آپ نے اپنا کام مکمل کیا؟
Where did she go?
وہ کہاں گئی؟

6. Common mistakes

  • Incorrect: I did not saw him. Correct: I did not see him.
  • Incorrect: Did he went home? Correct: Did he go home?
  • Incorrect: She go yesterday. Correct: She went yesterday.

Practice: test yourself

  1. Yesterday, we ___ (play) cricket.
  2. She ___ (go) to the shop last night.
  3. He didn't ___ (eat) breakfast.
  4. ___ you watch the match?
Show answers

1. played · 2. went · 3. eat · 4. Did

Speaking task: Say three things you did yesterday and one thing you did not do. Then write them in your notebook.
