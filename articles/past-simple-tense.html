Past Simple Tense in English with Urdu Examples

Learn how to talk about completed actions in the past using simple rules and examples.

This beginner-friendly lesson explains the topic step by step. Read each example, notice the pattern, and then make your own sentences. Learning a little every day is more effective than trying to memorise everything at once.

اردو میں: ہر مثال کو غور سے پڑھیں، اس کا مطلب سمجھیں اور پھر اپنی طرف سے ایک نیا جملہ بنائیں۔

When do we use the past simple?

Use the past simple for an action that started and finished in the past. Time expressions often include yesterday, last night, last week or two days ago.

Positive sentences

I visited my friend yesterday.
میں کل اپنے دوست سے ملا۔
She cooked dinner.
اس نے رات کا کھانا پکایا۔
We watched a film.
ہم نے ایک فلم دیکھی۔

Regular and irregular verbs

Many regular verbs take -ed: work → worked, clean → cleaned, play → played. Some common verbs change form: go → went, eat → ate, see → saw, buy → bought. Learn irregular verbs in small groups.

Negative sentences

Use did not (didn't) + the base form of the verb. Say “I did not go,” not “I did not went.”

He did not call me.
اس نے مجھے فون نہیں کیا۔
They didn't come to school.
وہ اسکول نہیں آئے۔

Questions

Use Did + subject + base verb?

Did you finish your work?
کیا آپ نے اپنا کام مکمل کیا؟
Where did she go?
وہ کہاں گئی؟

Quick check

Choose the correct form: “Yesterday I (play / played) cricket.” The correct answer is “played” because the action is finished in the past.

Practice task: Write five original sentences using the rules in this lesson. Read them aloud, then check your spelling and grammar.
