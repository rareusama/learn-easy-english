Present Continuous Tense in Urdu: Rules and Examples

Learn how to describe actions happening now or around the present time.

اردو میں: Present Continuous Tense ایسے کام کے لیے استعمال ہوتا ہے جو ابھی ہو رہا ہو یا ان دنوں جاری ہو۔

1. Sentence structure

Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing + object

  • I am reading a book. — میں کتاب پڑھ رہا ہوں۔
  • She is cooking dinner. — وہ کھانا پکا رہی ہے۔
  • They are playing cricket. — وہ کرکٹ کھیل رہے ہیں۔

2. Choose am, is or are

Am

Use with I: I am learning English.

Is

Use with he, she, it or one name: Ali is working.

Are

Use with you, we and they: We are studying.

3. Negative sentences

Add not after am/is/are.

  • I am not sleeping. — میں سو نہیں رہا ہوں۔
  • He is not watching TV. — وہ ٹی وی نہیں دیکھ رہا۔
  • We are not waiting. — ہم انتظار نہیں کر رہے۔

4. Questions

Move am/is/are before the subject.

  • Are you listening? — کیا تم سن رہے ہو؟
  • Is she coming? — کیا وہ آ رہی ہے؟
  • What are they doing? — وہ کیا کر رہے ہیں؟

5. Adding -ing

  • Most verbs: read → reading, play → playing.
  • Verbs ending in silent e: make → making, write → writing.
  • Some short verbs double the last consonant: run → running, sit → sitting.

6. When do we use it?

Use it for an action happening now (I am speaking), a temporary situation (She is staying with her aunt this week), or a changing situation (The weather is getting warmer).

Common mistakes

  • Correct: She is studying. Not: She studying.
  • Correct: They are playing. Not: They is playing.
  • Correct: He is writing. Not: He is write.

Practice

  1. I ___ (am/is/are) learning English.
  2. She is ___ (cook/cooking) now.
  3. ___ they coming? (Is/Are)
  4. He ___ not sleeping. (is/are)
Show answers

1. am · 2. cooking · 3. Are · 4. is

Speaking task: Look around and say five sentences about what people are doing. Example: “My brother is reading.”
