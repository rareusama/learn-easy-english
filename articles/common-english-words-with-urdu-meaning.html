How to learn vocabulary

  1. Learn five to ten words a day.
  2. Say each word aloud and read its Urdu meaning.
  3. Write one short English sentence using each new word.
  4. Review yesterday’s words before starting a new group.

Example: “Water is cold.” (پانی ٹھنڈا ہے۔) Try making your own sentence with “water,” “cold,” or another word from the list.

Practice task: Choose three examples from this lesson, write them in your notebook, and say them aloud. Try making one new sentence of your own.

Educational content only. Examples are for learning and practice.

Learn words through short sentences

Knowing a translation is a start, but using a word in a sentence helps you remember how it works.

  • Water: “Please give me a glass of water.”
  • Market: “My mother is going to the market.”
  • Important: “Practice is important for learning.”
  • Because: “I study because I want to improve.”

Some words have several meanings. For example, “work” can be a noun (“My work is interesting”) or a verb (“I work every day”). Learn the example as well as the Urdu meaning.
