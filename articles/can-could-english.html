How to Use Can and Could in English with Urdu

Use can and could to talk about ability, permission and polite requests.

آسان وضاحت: Can عموماً موجودہ صلاحیت یا اجازت کے لیے، جبکہ Could ماضی کی صلاحیت اور مؤدبانہ درخواست کے لیے استعمال ہوتا ہے۔

1. Can for present ability

Use can + base verb to say someone is able to do something.

I can swim.
میں تیر سکتا ہوں۔
She can speak English.
وہ انگریزی بول سکتی ہے۔

2. Can for permission and requests

Can I use your phone?
کیا میں آپ کا فون استعمال کر سکتا ہوں؟
Can you help me?
کیا آپ میری مدد کر سکتے ہیں؟

3. Could for past ability

Use could for a general ability someone had in the past.

When I was young, I could run fast.
جب میں چھوٹا تھا تو تیز دوڑ سکتا تھا۔

4. Could for polite requests

Could often sounds more polite or less direct than can.

Could you open the window, please?
کیا آپ براہِ کرم کھڑکی کھول سکتے ہیں؟
Could you repeat that?
کیا آپ اسے دوبارہ کہہ سکتے ہیں؟

5. Negative and question forms

Negative: cannot/can't and could not/couldn't. Questions: put can or could before the subject.

  • He can't drive.
  • Could they hear us?
  • Can she cook?

6. Important grammar rules

  • Use the base verb after can/could: “She can sing,” not “She can sings.”
  • Do not add “to”: “I can swim,” not “I can to swim.”
  • Can and could do not change for he, she or it.

Practice: complete the sentences

  1. My brother ___ ride a bicycle. (present ability)
  2. When I was five, I ___ not read.
  3. ___ you help me, please? (polite request)
  4. She can ___ (dance).
Show answers

1. can · 2. could · 3. Could · 4. dance

Speaking task: Say two things you can do, one thing you could do as a child, and one polite request using could.

Could has other uses too. This lesson focuses on common beginner meanings.
