10 Common English Mistakes Beginners Should Avoid

Small grammar changes can make your English clearer. Learn these common errors and practise the correct forms.

اردو میں: انگریزی سیکھتے وقت غلطیاں ہونا عام بات ہے۔ ہر غلطی سے ایک اصول سیکھیں اور درست جملہ دوبارہ بولیں۔

1. He go to school

Incorrect: He go to school. Correct: He goes to school.

Present simple tense mein he, she aur it ke saath verb mein aksar s/es lagta hai.

2. She don't like tea

Incorrect: She don't like tea. Correct: She doesn't like tea.

He, she, it ke saath negative present simple mein doesn't use hota hai. Doesn't ke baad base verb aata hai.

3. I am agree

Incorrect: I am agree. Correct: I agree.

Agree khud verb hai; is se pehle am ki zaroorat nahi.

4. He is more taller

Incorrect: He is more taller than me. Correct: He is taller than me.

Short adjectives ke comparative form mein aam tor par -er use hota hai; “more” aur “-er” ko saath na lagayen.

5. I have 20 years

Incorrect: I have 20 years. Correct: I am 20 years old.

Age batane ke liye English mein “be” verb use hota hai.

6. Discuss about the problem

Incorrect: Let's discuss about the problem. Correct: Let's discuss the problem.

Discuss ke baad direct object aata hai; “about” ki zaroorat nahi.

7. Return back

Incorrect: Please return back soon. Correct: Please return soon.

Return ka matlab hi wapas aana ya jana hai, is liye “back” aksar extra hota hai.

8. I didn't went

Incorrect: I didn't went there. Correct: I didn't go there.

Did/didn't ke baad verb ki base form use hoti hai.

9. She can sings

Incorrect: She can sings. Correct: She can sing.

Can, could, will aur doosre modal verbs ke baad base verb aata hai.

10. An university

Incorrect: an university. Correct: a university.

A/an ka faisla spelling se nahi, pehli sound se hota hai. University ki shuruat “yu” sound se hoti hai.

Quick revision quiz

  1. She ___ English every day. (study/studies)
  2. They didn't ___ yesterday. (come/came)
  3. I ___ 18 years old. (am/have)
  4. He can ___. (swim/swims)
  5. ___ honest person. (A/An)
Show answers

1. studies · 2. come · 3. am · 4. swim · 5. An
