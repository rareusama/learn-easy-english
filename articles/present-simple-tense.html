Present Simple Tense in Urdu: Rules and Examples

Understand the present simple tense with easy rules, examples and Urdu explanations.

The present simple tense is used for habits, routines, facts and things that are generally true. It is one of the most useful tenses for beginners.

اردو میں: اس سبق کو آہستہ آہستہ پڑھیں، مثالوں کو بلند آواز سے دہرائیں اور روزانہ مشق کریں۔

1. Positive sentences

I play cricket.
میں کرکٹ کھیلتا ہوں۔
She reads a book.
وہ کتاب پڑھتی ہے۔
They work every day.
وہ ہر روز کام کرتے ہیں۔

2. Basic rule

With I, you, we and they, use the base form of the verb. With he, she and it, usually add s or es to the verb. For example: I work / He works.

3. Negative sentences

I do not like tea.
مجھے چائے پسند نہیں ہے۔
He does not play football.
وہ فٹ بال نہیں کھیلتا۔

4. Questions

Do you speak English?
کیا آپ انگریزی بولتے ہیں؟
Does she live here?
کیا وہ یہاں رہتی ہے؟

Spelling rules for he, she and it

Most verbs take -s: work → works, read → reads. Add -es to verbs ending in -sh, -ch, -ss, -x or -o: wash → washes, watch → watches, go → goes. For a consonant + y, change y to -ies: study → studies. But vowel + y takes -s: play → plays.

Adverbs of frequency

Words such as always, usually, often, sometimes and never tell us how often something happens. They usually come before the main verb: “I usually walk to school.” With the verb be, they usually come after it: “She is always kind.”

Common mistakes

  • Correct: He plays cricket. Not: He play cricket.
  • Correct: She does not like tea. Not: She does not likes tea.
  • Correct: Does he work here? Not: Do he work here?

Practice: choose the correct answer

  1. My brother (go / goes) to school every day.
  2. They (do not / does not) eat meat.
  3. (Do / Does) your mother speak English?
  4. She (study / studies) in the evening.

Answers: goes; do not; Does; studies. Remember: after does or does not, use the base verb.

Practice task: Choose three examples from this lesson, write them in your notebook, and say them aloud. Try making one new sentence of your own.
