A, An and The in English Grammar with Urdu

Learn when to use a, an and the before English nouns.

آسان وضاحت: A، An اور The اسم (noun) سے پہلے آتے ہیں۔ A اور An کسی عام چیز کے لیے، جبکہ The کسی خاص یا معلوم چیز کے لیے استعمال ہوتا ہے۔

1. What are articles?

English has three common articles: a, an and the. Use a/an with a singular countable noun when it is not a particular one. Use the when the noun is specific or already known.

2. Use “a”

Use a before a singular countable noun that begins with a consonant sound.

a book · a car · a teacher
ایک کتاب · ایک گاڑی · ایک استاد
a university
ایک یونیورسٹی — آغاز کی آواز “یُو” ہے۔

3. Use “an”

Use an before a singular countable noun that begins with a vowel sound.

an apple · an egg · an idea
ایک سیب · ایک انڈا · ایک خیال
an hour
ایک گھنٹہ — h خاموش ہے، اس لیے آغاز vowel sound سے ہوتا ہے۔

Important: Choose by sound, not just by the first letter. We say “a university” but “an hour”.

4. Use “the”

Use the when you mean a particular thing, when it has already been mentioned, or when the context makes it clear.

I saw a dog. The dog was black.
میں نے ایک کتا دیکھا۔ وہ کتا کالا تھا۔
Please close the door.
براہِ کرم دروازہ بند کر دیں۔
The sun is bright.
سورج روشن ہے۔

5. When no article is usually needed

Do not normally use a/an before plural nouns or uncountable nouns when speaking generally: “Books are useful” and “Water is important.” Do not say “a books” or “a water” in these meanings.

6. Common mistakes

  • Incorrect: an university. Correct: a university.
  • Incorrect: a apple. Correct: an apple.
  • Incorrect: I like the music (when speaking generally about music). Often: I like music.

  5. I bought a bag. ___ bag is blue.
