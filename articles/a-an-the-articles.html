A, An and The in English Grammar with Urdu

Understand when to use a, an and the with clear beginner examples and Urdu meanings.

This beginner-friendly lesson explains the topic step by step. Read each example, notice the pattern, and then make your own sentences. Learning a little every day is more effective than trying to memorise everything at once.

اردو میں: ہر مثال کو غور سے پڑھیں، اس کا مطلب سمجھیں اور پھر اپنی طرف سے ایک نیا جملہ بنائیں۔

What are articles?

A, an and the are small words used before nouns. A and an are indefinite articles. The is the definite article.

Use “a”

Use “a” before a singular countable noun that begins with a consonant sound.

a book
ایک کتاب
a car
ایک گاڑی
a university
ایک یونیورسٹی (یہاں آغاز کی آواز “یُو” ہے)

Use “an”

Use “an” before a singular countable noun that begins with a vowel sound.

an apple
ایک سیب
an egg
ایک انڈا
an hour
ایک گھنٹہ (h خاموش ہے)

Use “the”

Use “the” when the listener knows which person or thing you mean, or when you mean something specific.

Please close the door.
براہِ کرم دروازہ بند کر دیں۔
The sun is bright today.
آج سورج روشن ہے۔

Common mistakes

Do not choose a or an only by the first letter; listen to the first sound. Also, do not normally use a or an with plural nouns or uncountable nouns: say “books” and “water,” not “a books” or “a water.”

Quick practice

Fill the blanks: ___ orange; ___ pen; ___ moon. Answers: an orange, a pen, the moon.

Practice task: Write five original sentences using the rules in this lesson. Read them aloud, then check your spelling and grammar.
