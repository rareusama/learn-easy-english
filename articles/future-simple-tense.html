Future Simple Tense in Urdu: Will and Won't

Talk about future actions, promises, quick decisions and predictions.

اردو میں: Future Simple Tense مستقبل میں ہونے والے کام یا فیصلے کے بارے میں بات کرنے کے لیے استعمال ہوتا ہے۔

1. Basic structure

Subject + will + base verb

  • I will call you. — میں تمہیں فون کروں گا۔
  • She will help us. — وہ ہماری مدد کرے گی۔
  • They will arrive tomorrow. — وہ کل پہنچیں گے۔

Use the base form after will: “He will go,” not “He will goes.” Will stays the same with I, you, he, she, it, we and they.

2. Negative sentences

Use will not or the short form won't.

  • I will not forget. — میں نہیں بھولوں گا۔
  • He won't come today. — وہ آج نہیں آئے گا۔
  • We won't be late. — ہم دیر سے نہیں ہوں گے۔

3. Questions

Put will before the subject.

  • Will you join us? — کیا تم ہمارے ساتھ شامل ہو گے؟
  • Will it rain today? — کیا آج بارش ہوگی؟
  • When will they arrive? — وہ کب پہنچیں گے؟

4. Common uses of will

  • Quick decision: The phone is ringing. I will answer it.
  • Promise: I will help you with your homework.
  • Offer: I will carry that bag for you.
  • Prediction: I think our team will win.

5. Will and going to

Both can talk about the future. Will is common for quick decisions, offers and predictions. “Going to” is often used for a plan already made or a prediction based on present evidence: “I am going to visit Lahore next week.”

Common mistakes

  • Correct: She will study. Not: She will studies.
  • Correct: Will he come? Not: Does he will come?
  • Correct: I won't go. Not: I won't to go.

Practice

  1. We ___ visit our teacher tomorrow. (will/wills)
  2. She will ___ you later. (call/calls)
  3. ___ you help me? (Will/Are)
  4. He ___ come today. (won't/don't)
Show answers

1. will · 2. call · 3. Will · 4. won't
