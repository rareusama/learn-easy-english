English Prepositions: In, On and At with Urdu

Learn the basic use of in, on and at for time and place with practical examples.

This beginner-friendly lesson explains the topic step by step. Read each example, notice the pattern, and then make your own sentences. Learning a little every day is more effective than trying to memorise everything at once.

اردو میں: ہر مثال کو غور سے پڑھیں، اس کا مطلب سمجھیں اور پھر اپنی طرف سے ایک نیا جملہ بنائیں۔

What is a preposition?

A preposition shows a relationship, often about time or place. In, on and at are common prepositions, but their use depends on the meaning.

Use “in” for larger periods and enclosed places

in July / in 2026 / in the morning
جولائی میں / 2026 میں / صبح کے وقت
The keys are in the bag.
چابیاں بیگ کے اندر ہیں۔
She lives in Lahore.
وہ لاہور میں رہتی ہے۔

Use “on” for days, dates and surfaces

on Monday / on 5 May
پیر کے دن / 5 مئی کو
The phone is on the table.
فون میز پر ہے۔

Use “at” for exact times and specific points

at 7 o'clock / at noon
سات بجے / دوپہر کے وقت
Meet me at the bus stop.
مجھ سے بس اسٹاپ پر ملیں۔

Easy way to remember

For time, think of in as a longer period, on as a day or date, and at as an exact time. For place, in often means inside an area, on means on a surface, and at points to a particular location.

Quick practice

Choose: “My birthday is ___ June.” Answer: in. “The class starts ___ 9:00.” Answer: at. “We meet ___ Friday.” Answer: on.

Practice task: Write five original sentences using the rules in this lesson. Read them aloud, then check your spelling and grammar.
