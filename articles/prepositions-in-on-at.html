English Prepositions: In, On and At with Urdu

Understand the basic difference between in, on and at for time and place.

آسان وضاحت: In، On اور At وقت اور جگہ کے تعلق کو ظاہر کرتے ہیں۔ ان کا درست استعمال مثالوں سے سیکھیں۔

1. Use “in” for longer periods and areas

Time: in July, in 2026, in winter, in the morning.

Place: in a room, in a bag, in Lahore, in Pakistan.

The keys are in the bag.
چابیاں بیگ کے اندر ہیں۔

2. Use “on” for days, dates and surfaces

Time: on Monday, on 5 May, on my birthday.

Place: on the table, on the wall, on the floor.

The phone is on the table.
فون میز پر ہے۔

3. Use “at” for exact times and specific points

Time: at 7 o'clock, at noon, at night.

Place: at the bus stop, at the door, at school.

Meet me at the bus stop at 6.
مجھ سے چھ بجے بس اسٹاپ پر ملیں۔

4. Compare the three

in the morning
صبح کے وقت
on Monday morning
پیر کی صبح
at 8 in the morning
صبح آٹھ بجے

5. Useful exceptions

We usually say at night, but in the morning / afternoon / evening. We say on the weekend in American English and often at the weekend in British English.

  2. The class starts ___ 9:00.

  4. The picture is ___ the wall.
  5. She lives ___ Lahore.
  6. I study ___ the evening.

Practice task: Write three sentences about your daily routine using in, on and at.

These are common beginner patterns. Some expressions are fixed and need to be learned as phrases.

Compare in, on and at in a daily routine

“I wake up at 7:00 in the morning. I have an English class on Monday. The class is in Room 4, and I wait at the door.”

Think of the common pattern: at often points to an exact time or point, on to a day or surface, and in to a longer period or an enclosed area. These are useful patterns, but fixed expressions such as “at night” should be learned as complete phrases.
